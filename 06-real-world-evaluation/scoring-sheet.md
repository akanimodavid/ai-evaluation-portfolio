## 3. Key Findings

### Accuracy
Accuracy was the weakest area, with an average score of 3.4/5.

The most significant issue occurred in P3, where ChatGPT provided the correct compound interest formula but calculated the wrong final amount. P1 also contained an incorrect budget allocation. P2 and P5 included financial claims or recommendations that needed additional qualification.

### Instruction Following
ChatGPT performed relatively well, with an average score of 4.2/5.

It followed most of the requested instructions. However, P1 contained an inconsistent budget allocation, and P4 directly violated the negative word constraint.

### Relevance & Completeness
ChatGPT performed strongly, with an average score of 4.8/5.

Its responses generally addressed the user's questions and provided relevant information. P5 could have included more specific guidance on calculating an appropriate emergency fund.

### Clarity
ChatGPT scored 5/5 across all five prompts.

The responses were organised, readable, and understandable. The model used tables, numbered lists, and straightforward explanations where appropriate.

### Safety
ChatGPT achieved an average score of 4.8/5.

It handled the bankruptcy question responsibly by avoiding specific legal instructions and recommending professional assistance. However, some financial recommendations needed additional context and qualification.

## 4. Overall Assessment

ChatGPT demonstrated strong performance across the five tested personal finance scenarios, particularly in clarity, relevance, and safety.

However, the evaluation identified weaknesses in mathematical accuracy, strict instruction following, and the qualification of financial information.

The P3 calculation error was particularly important because the model presented a confident numerical answer despite using an incorrect result. This demonstrates why AI-generated calculations should be independently verified, even when the explanation appears mathematically sound.

The results also show that a response can be clear and relevant while still containing factual or numerical errors.

## 5. Limitations

- The evaluation covered only five selected prompts.
- The results represent ChatGPT's responses at the time of testing.
- The prompts do not cover every aspect of personal finance.
- The scoring involved one primary human evaluator.
- The evaluation does not establish that ChatGPT is reliable for professional financial advice.

## 6. Conclusion

This evaluation demonstrated that ChatGPT can produce clear, relevant, and generally responsible responses to everyday personal finance questions. However, it also showed that strong presentation does not guarantee factual or mathematical accuracy.

The most important finding was the model's failure to calculate compound interest correctly in P3, despite providing the correct formula.

The evaluation reinforces the importance of assessing AI responses against clearly defined criteria rather than judging them by confidence, readability, or apparent usefulness alone.
