Humans need sleep because it gives the body and brain time to rest, recover, and prepare for the next day. Sleep is not simply switching off; important activities continue happening while we sleep.

### Why Do Humans Need Sleep?

There are five main reasons:

1. **Brain recovery:** Sleep helps the brain process information, organize memories, and improve learning.
2. **Physical repair:** The body repairs muscles, tissues, and cells while we sleep.
3. **Energy restoration:** Sleep helps restore energy so we can function properly throughout the day.
4. **Immune system support:** Sleep strengthens the immune system, helping the body fight infections and illnesses.
5. **Emotional balance:** Getting enough sleep helps control emotions, reduce stress, and maintain a stable mood.

### What Happens When We Don't Get Enough Sleep?

When someone regularly gets insufficient sleep, several parts of the body begin to suffer.

- **Brain:** Concentration, memory, decision-making, and reaction time become worse.
- **Heart:** Poor sleep can increase blood pressure and raise the risk of heart disease.
- **Immune system:** The body becomes less effective at fighting infections.
- **Mental health:** Sleep deprivation can increase irritability, anxiety, and the risk of depression.
- **Metabolism:** It can affect hormones that control hunger, making someone feel hungrier and increasing the risk of weight gain.
- **Physical performance:** Muscles may feel weaker, and the body takes longer to recover from physical activities.

### How Much Sleep Do Humans Need?

The amount of sleep needed depends on age:

| Age Group | Recommended Sleep |
|---|---|
| Newborns | 14–17 hours |
| Babies (4–12 months) | 12–16 hours |
| Children (1–5 years) | 10–14 hours |
| School-age children (6–12 years) | 9–12 hours |
| Teenagers (13–17 years) | 8–10 hours |
| Adults (18–64 years) | 7–9 hours |
| Older adults (65+) | 7–8 hours |

### What Happens If You Stay Awake for Too Long?

The effects become more serious the longer someone stays awake.

- **After 24 hours:** Concentration, judgment, and coordination can become severely impaired.
- **After 36 hours:** Extreme tiredness, poor memory, and difficulty making decisions become more noticeable.
- **After 48 hours:** People may experience involuntary brief sleep episodes called microsleeps, and some may experience hallucinations.

### In Simple Terms

Think of sleep as maintenance for the human body. Just as a computer needs maintenance to keep working properly, the human body needs sleep to repair itself, organize information, restore energy, and maintain good health.

**The bottom line:** Sleep is essential for survival. Without enough sleep, both the brain and body gradually lose their ability to function properly.
