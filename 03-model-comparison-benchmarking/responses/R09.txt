**Step 1: Calculate the profit.**

Profit = Selling Price − Cost Price

= ₦10,000 − ₦8,000

= **₦2,000**

**Step 2: Calculate the percentage profit.**

Percentage Profit = (Profit ÷ Cost Price) × 100

= (₦2,000 ÷ ₦8,000) × 100

= 0.25 × 100

= **25%**

**Final Answer: 25% profit.**

The shopkeeper makes a profit of ₦2,000, which is 25% of the original purchase price.
