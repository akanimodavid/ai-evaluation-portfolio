**Photosynthesis is the process by which plants make their own food using sunlight.**

Think of a plant as a tiny food factory. Instead of buying or eating food like humans do, plants produce their own food.

Here's how it works:

### 🌱 The 4 Things a Plant Needs

1. **Sunlight:** The plant gets energy from the sun.
2. **Water:** The roots absorb water from the soil.
3. **Carbon dioxide:** The leaves take in carbon dioxide from the air.
4. **Chlorophyll:** This is the green substance in leaves that helps capture sunlight.

### ☀️ How Photosynthesis Happens

1. The roots absorb water from the soil.
2. The leaves take in carbon dioxide from the air.
3. The leaves capture energy from sunlight using chlorophyll.
4. The plant uses these ingredients to produce glucose (sugar), which it uses as food.
5. Oxygen is released into the air.

All inputs are high, so the photosynthesis rate is high

### 🧠 Remember This

Photosynthesis produces two main things:

- **Glucose:** Food that provides energy for the plant.
- **Oxygen:** Gas released into the atmosphere that humans and animals breathe.

### Simple Way to Understand It

Imagine a plant standing in the sun:

- Its roots drink water.
- Its leaves breathe in carbon dioxide.
- Sunlight provides energy.
- The plant makes its own food.
- Oxygen comes out.

**That's photosynthesis!**
