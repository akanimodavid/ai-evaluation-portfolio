The percentage profit is **25%**. 

Here is the step-by-step explanation of the calculation:

1. **Identify the Cost Price (CP) and Selling Price (SP):**
   - Cost Price (the amount the shopkeeper bought the item for) = ₦8,000
   - Selling Price (the amount the shopkeeper sold the item for) = ₦10,000

2. **Calculate the Profit:**
   - Profit = Selling Price - Cost Price
   - Profit = ₦10,000 - ₦8,000 = **₦2,000**

3. **Calculate the Percentage Profit:**
   - The formula for percentage profit is: `(Profit / Cost Price) × 100`
   - Percentage Profit = (₦2,000 / ₦8,000) × 100
   - Percentage Profit = 0.25 × 100 = **25%**

So, the shopkeeper made a 25% profit on the item.
