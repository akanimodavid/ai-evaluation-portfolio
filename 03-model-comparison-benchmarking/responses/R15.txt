Yes, some plants can grow without direct sunlight, but most plants need light to grow properly.

Plants use sunlight to make their food through a process called **photosynthesis**. Without enough light, most plants cannot produce the energy they need to grow and stay healthy.

However, some plants can survive in low-light conditions, and others can grow using artificial light, such as LED grow lights.

**In short:** Most plants need light to grow, but it does not always have to come directly from the sun.
